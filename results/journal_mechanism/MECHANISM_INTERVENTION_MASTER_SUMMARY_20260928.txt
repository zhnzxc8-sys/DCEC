============================================================
CURRENT SAFE MECHANISTIC CONCLUSION
============================================================

The intervention evidence supports the functional importance of the learned
correction geometry. Across gaussian blur and pixelation, reversing or
randomizing correction direction degrades retrieval, while excessive scaling
causes over-correction. Correction shuffling further indicates image-specific
matching.

The exact per-image five-dimensional quality descriptor is not supported as the
dominant mechanism under the tested blur condition.

These findings are functional intervention evidence. They should not be
presented as universal causal claims without broader intervention coverage.
